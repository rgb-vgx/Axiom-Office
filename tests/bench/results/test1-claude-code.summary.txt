## Two things worth flagging

1. **The UNO bridge quirks.** This LibreOffice build requires `;` as the formula argument separator (commas silently produce `#NAME?`/`#Err:508`), and it rejects both `XSheetConditionalEntries.addNew` and chart `Diagram` assignment. Conditional formatting and the line-chart conversion are therefore applied by rewriting the exported OOXML — then verified by re-opening the file in Calc (CF reads back as 16 blocks/45 rules, 5 charts, 0 error cells).

2. **Bugs I found and fixed during verification**, rather than shipping around them: self-referencing budget formulas, an off-by-one column shift in the scenario totals, and a KPI "% of Total" that divided by a cell on the wrong sheet (North America read 299% instead of 50.8%).
